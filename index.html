01 · The profile today

126 posts in, the profile still doesn’t sell.

A buyer who likes a clip lands here next. This screen tells them what you are, and nothing else.

  • Handle carries a trailing underscore. @wizcommerce_ reads as the backup account, not the brand.
  • Name field is one word. It is the only field Instagram search indexes, spent on a word nobody searches.
  • Bio is one descriptive line. No proof, no next step.
  • Five links behind one tinyurl. “and 4 more” asks a buyer to choose. Most choose nothing.
  • No highlights at all. The row below the buttons is empty, so the grid starts cold.
  • Grid opens mid-sentence. “Everything,” “Their Territory” “we cannot” are auto-caption fragments, not hooks.
