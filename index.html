01 · The profile

126 posts in, the profile still doesn’t sell.

A buyer who likes a clip lands here next. Right now this screen tells them what you are, and almost nothing else. Five fixable things, none of which need a designer.

  • Handle carries a trailing underscore. @wizcommerce_ reads as the backup account, not the brand. Chase @wizcommerce; if it is held, @wizcommerceAI is the clean fallback.
  • Name field is one word. “WizCommerce” alone. That field is the only part of a profile Instagram search actually indexes, and it is being spent on a word nobody searches.
  • Bio is a single descriptive line. no proof, no next step.
  • Five links behind one tinyurl. “and 4 more” asks a buyer to choose. Most choose nothing.
  • No highlights at all. the row below the buttons is empty, so the grid starts cold.
126 posts of work pointing at a profile that asks for nothing.

05 · The finding

Two of the three open on a title card.

A viewer decides in about a second. Both of these spend that second on a logo animation, before a word is said or a face appears. The branding is genuinely good. It is just placed where it costs the most.

  • 10 Aug, 428 views — static title card, then the guest.
  • 11 Sep, 179 views — same card, and this one is a collab with @crystorama. Borrowed reach, spent on an intro.
  • 30 Jul — opens on the guest, no title card. The strongest of the three openings, and it carries no text at all.
A collab reaching 2.4× fewer people than a solo post is the intro doing damage.

06 · The text

The best line is in the caption, where nobody reads it.

@wizcommerce_ · 30 Jul 2026 · on-screen text reads “are they”

That post’s caption opens with “Your rep carries 15 lines. Why would they lead with yours?” — a sharp, specific question that would stop the right buyer cold. On screen, the viewer instead gets a two-word fragment of a running auto-caption.

  • The hook is spoken, so anyone scrolling on mute gets nothing.
  • The clip starts mid-thought: “because the industry is reliant on independent reps” never says who this is for.
  • Newer posts do carry styled text. It is inconsistent, not absent.
Order that works: hook broad, body narrow, CTA specific.
07 / 08
07 · What we would change

Four changes, none of them a reshoot.

The footage is good and the guests are right. This is all post-production, applied to clips you have already made.

Lead with the face, not the card

Move the branded intro to the end, or cut it to under half a second. Let the guest’s strongest sentence be frame one.

Put the hook on screen

A written hook that adds the context the audio assumes. Not a transcript — the line that tells a wholesaler this is about them.

Show it, don’t only say it

B-roll and light motion graphics behind claims. Product, dashboard, warehouse. Supports the words instead of repeating them.

Cut the zoom rhythm back to punctuation

The minimal, premium look is right for a corporate buyer. Constant push-in and pull-out works against it. Keep the move for the line that deserves emphasis, and hold the frame everywhere else. Used on every sentence, it stops reading as emphasis and starts reading as noise.
